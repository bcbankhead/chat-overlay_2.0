# TDF Chat 2.0 — UI design (2026-10-27)

Settled in a hands-on session against a saved copy of the live chat, with the overlay
injected as Tampermonkey would. Every decision below was made by looking at it working.
The working mockup is `reference/mockup/` (see its README to run it). Where this document
and the mockup disagree, this document wins; where this document and an older ticket plan
disagree, this document wins and the plan is revised.

Ticket mapping is in the last section.

## Global

- **Two themes, light and dark,** switched by a toggle (not a three-way auto/light/dark
  cycle). The switch cross-fades every colour over 300 ms (background, text, borders); the
  fade applies only during a switch, so hovers and slides keep their own timing.
- **Page background:** light `#FBFBFF`, dark `#DADAE6`. The engine's background image is
  removed.
- **Rounded corners, 10 px,** on the message view, the sidebar and every flyout.
- **Floating scrollbars** on the message view, the flyouts and the user lists: native bars
  hidden; a thin rounded thumb drawn over the content, shown while scrolling or hovering,
  fading about a second after; wider under the pointer; draggable. The message box keeps
  its scrolling with its bar hidden (a textarea cannot hold a floating thumb).
- **Flyouts** grow leftward from the sidebar's left edge, all the same width (three 128 px
  columns + gaps + padding, about 418 px), and are as tall as their content up to the
  sidebar's height. One open at a time. Opening slides in (about 220 ms) and fades; the
  contents fade in once the slide ends. **The first click outside an open flyout only
  closes it** — that click does nothing else. Esc closes too. Reduced-motion skips the
  animations.

## Message view

- **Layout: avatar + grouped.** A run of public messages from one sender shows the avatar,
  name and time once; the rest of the run is indented beneath. A gap of more than 5 minutes
  starts a new group. Avatars are **48×48 rounded squares (8 px radius)**; a one-message
  group is held to the avatar's height so avatars never touch.
- **Join, leave and system notices** are small muted lines at the text indent. They never
  take a sender colour.
- **Fonts are ours.** Every message uses the UI font at the view's size; the sender's font
  family and size are ignored. (This reverses the parent Shared Contract's "sender font
  family preserved".)
- **Sender colour is kept, adapted per theme — the contrast chip is removed.** Rule, on the
  theme's background:
  1. Contrast already ≥ 4.5:1 (WCAG) → shown as chosen.
  2. Otherwise move its OKLab lightness away from the background, keeping hue and chroma,
     by the smallest amount that reaches 4.5:1 (search target 4.6 to survive rounding to
     hex). Chroma is reduced only as far as needed to stay in sRGB.
  3. A grey (OKLab chroma < 0.02) that fails → the theme's own text colour.
  Measured: `#003366` → `#5585be` on dark; `#ff69b4` → `#cd3a89` on light; `#000000` →
  theme text on dark. The background used is the theme's token (`--tdf-bg`), never the
  computed background, which is mid-fade during a theme switch.
- **Posted images** (larger than 48 px; smilies excluded) show a pointer and fade to 80%
  opacity on hover. A link wrapping an image is sized to the image and never takes the
  link-hover background (it showed through the fade as a bar).
- **Image reactions (TDF-0011, TDF-0014):** hovering a posted image shows a small heart
  in its upper-right corner that likes it; on a device without hover it is always shown.
  Clicking the image itself opens it full size in a new tab; it keeps the pointer cursor
  and its hover fade, and right-clicking opens the browser's own menu. The corner heart
  is a white outline that fills red while pointed at, and once you like the image it
  stays filled red with no outline and shows without hover; a queued or sending
  like is filled and dimmed, and a failed one is dashed and retries on click. Your own
  images have no corner heart. Up to three hearts stack at three angles in the image's
  lower-left corner, one per distinct liker across any smiley (other installs may still
  react with one), you included; there is no count and no liker is named. Likes are
  permanent: there is no take-back, and liking an image you already reacted to sends
  nothing. Floating hearts rise from the image's bottom edge when your like is queued and
  when another user's like arrives live (not at bootstrap or rebuild); none play under
  reduced motion. In the product a reaction is sent as TDF-0009.6 plans: the smilie as a
  message right after the image, queued 15 s apart, inferred by every install. A like
  sends `:h` with a hidden `^suffix` token in a background-coloured span, which the 2.0
  view never shows.

## Sidebar

- **Background:** light `#EDEDF5`, dark `#27272E`. Chips one tint lighter (dark
  `#32323B`, border `#43434E`).
- **Icon row:** Gallery, Profile, Light/Dark toggle (moon in light, sun in dark) on the
  left; **Settings (gear) anchored right**, the free space being the gap.
- **Current room:** a chip-accordion (name, count, caret), open by default, with its user
  list beneath (24×24 rounded-square avatars, 5 px radius). It fills the remaining height.
- **Other rooms:** chip-accordions at the bottom, collapsed by default.
- **Logout:** a red outline button under the last room (red text and border `#e5484d`,
  translucent red fill on hover). It presses the engine's own Logout.
- **Room creation is gone:** the engine's room icon, create form and room dropdown are
  hidden; the sidebar grows up into their space.
- **A user's name** (left or right click) opens a small menu, opening leftward from the
  pointer: **Whisper**, or **Stop whispering** on the user already whispered to; disabled
  on your own name. It replaces the engine's Whisper To field.

## Flyouts

- **Gallery:** a sticky header (title, `N images · size on disk`, a star toggle that
  filters to favourites; the size's tooltip splits originals and thumbnails). A 3-column
  grid of **128×128** thumbnails, made once on a canvas (centre crop, WebP) and stored, so
  opening never loads full images. Each tile: **trash at top left, star at top right**,
  shown on hover (a starred tile keeps its star). Star = favourite: protected from
  deletion (refused by storage, not just the UI) and kept by the filter. Trash deletes
  permanently. A chip at the **lower left** shows the image's size on disk, always visible,
  its text orange from 1 MB (TDF-0024). Thumbnails fade in after the flyout's slide ends.
- **Profile:** your avatar and name, **status (Here / BRB / Away)**, eCredits (opens the
  engine's eCredits box), then **Text style**: a preview line and two accordions, closed
  by default — **Font** (the engine's 11 families, each in its own face; header shows the
  current one) and **Colour** (the engine's 108 swatches, 12 per row; header shows a
  swatch). Choices go to the engine's own `textFamily` / `textColor`.
- **Settings (gear):** **Chat** — Auto-scroll switch (bound to the engine's checkbox),
  Clear chat buffer (empties the view; saved history is kept and returns on reload).
  **Storage** — Chat history (days; default 30; 0 keeps none; oldest deleted first, on
  load and on change); Clear history now (deletes every saved message, in every room);
  Delete messages by user (a picker of every sender in saved history, online or not, with
  their message count, and a Delete button); and Gallery size limit (MB; empty = no limit;
  oldest non-favourites deleted first). Under each field a hint says what the typed value
  would delete; the deletion runs only when the value is committed. The two deleting
  buttons confirm inline: the first click shows "Delete N messages?" with Confirm and
  Cancel, and only Confirm deletes. Neither touches the gallery; tags derived from the
  deleted messages go with them.

## Composer

- **The engine's icon row is replaced by one outline smiley button** in its place; it opens
  a 5-wide picker of the engine's 15 smilies, inserting the code. The other icons are gone:
  Clear Screen moved to Settings; Text Options to Profile; the rest are dropped.
- **Bottom bar removed:** Whisper To, AutoScroll and eCredits leave the composer (to the
  user menu, Settings and Profile). The engine's controls stay in the page, hidden.
- **Message box:** 75 px tall, 6 px corners, the message view's own background in both
  themes (never the contrast chip; your colour is shifted by the same rule as messages).
  Border: dark — the box's own colour (invisible); light — the default. Cursor colour white
  on dark, black on light (default width).
- **Container:** light `#DADAE6`, dark `#444450`.
- **Send:** solid green `#2da44e`, white text, the box's height and corners.
- **Hint line, always shown,** in the box's lower right: "Shift+Enter for newline · Drag and
  drop images", small grey. The box keeps a bottom strip clear for it.
- **Upload:** a grey upload icon, no background, at the right edge slightly above centre.
  Images can also be dropped on the box. A pending image shows as a 44 px thumbnail at the
  box's left, where text starts (text moves over); its × removes it. Enter or Send posts
  it. (The mockup posts locally; the upload route — the engine's Share Files plugin — is
  for the ticket to settle.)
- **Whisper indicator:** while whispering, the box gets a blue border (`#2f81f7`) and a blue
  chip "Whisper <name> ×" sits on the box's **top border at the right**; × stops
  whispering.

## Engine facts gathered (see `reference/mockup/notes/toolbar-menus.md`)

- Toolbar menus are mostly client-built from `settings.js.php` (smilies, colours, fonts,
  sizes, sounds, avatars); Message History and Share Files are server windows; Help is
  `help/index.php` (Pro Chat Rooms v8.4.2), documenting `/roll`, `/me`, `/broadcast`,
  YouTube links and the (A)/(M)/(S) badges.
- `whisperUser()` / `clearWhisper()` are the only writers of `#whisperID` and assign
  `.value` directly (no input event).
- Unicode emoji survive the live chat as text.
